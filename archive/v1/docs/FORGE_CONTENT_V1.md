## WEEK 1 — BUILDING YOUR FOUNDATION

### Day 1 — Mon: Building your foundation (220 XP)

**Mission 1.1 — Communication**
- **Type:** Scenario
- **Source:** SSB
- **XP:** 50
- **Content:** You're in a college group project. Your idea gets ignored by the team. The deadline is tomorrow. What do you do?

**Mission 1.2 — Confidence**
- **Type:** Reflect & Write
- **Source:** Daily life
- **XP:** 40
- **Content:** Think of one moment this week where you held back from saying something you believed was right. Why did you stay quiet — and what would you say now?

**Mission 1.3 — Leadership**
- **Type:** Rapid Fire
- **Source:** SSB
- **XP:** 30
- **Content:** RAPID FIRE — You have 20 seconds: Name one Indian military leader who demonstrated exceptional leadership under crisis and state what specifically they did.

**Mission 1.4 — Awareness**
- **Type:** Poll + Reasoning
- **Source:** SSB
- **XP:** 40
- **Content:** POLL: Should India increase its defence budget to 3% of GDP (from ~2%)? Pick a side and give your strongest argument in 2 sentences.

**Mission 1.5 — Officer Thinking**
- **Type:** Daily Challenge
- **Source:** Daily life
- **XP:** 60
- **Content:** REAL WORLD TASK: Today, when someone gives you a task or instruction, before doing it — pause and ask: 'What is the intent behind this, not just the task itself?' Note what you discover.
