### Current Balance Assessment:
✅ **GOOD:** 1-day Officer promotion provides early achievement

- Day 1 → Day 2 retention rate
- Day 2 → Day 3 retention rate
- Whether users find 3-day Commander progression satisfying

## CONCLUSION

The current XP economy provides:
- **Fast early reward:** Officer rank on Day 1 (retention boost)
- **Clear short-term goal:** Commander rank on Day 3
- **Consistent daily value:** 520 XP per day is predictable and fair

**Recommendation:** Launch with current values. Measure retention metrics before adjusting.
