## CONTENT QUALITY ISSUES IDENTIFIED

### 🔴 CRITICAL ISSUES

#### 1. EXCESSIVE REPETITION
**Problem:** WAT and TAT appear too many times across sessions

**Occurrences:**
- Session 1: Q5 (TAT), Q9 (WAT)
- Session 2: Q1 (WAT), Q10 (TAT), Q11 (WAT count), Q12 (WAT timing)
- Session 3: Q5-Q7 (WAT style)

**Total Mentions:** 8 times (WAT: 6, TAT: 2)

**Impact:** User may feel "This again?" by Session 2-3

**Recommendation:** 
- Session 1: Keep Q5 (TAT) and Q9 (WAT) as introduction
- Session 2: Remove Q1 (WAT acronym) → Replace with GTO obstacle question
- Session 2: Keep Q11-Q12 (numeric facts about WAT)
- Session 3: WAT-style questions are authentic practice (keep)

#### 2. OLQ OVEREMPHASIS
**Problem:** OLQ concept repeated across all sessions

**Occurrences:**
- Session 1: Q4 (What is OLQ?), Q10 (most important quality)
- Session 2: Q3 (OLQ list check), Q9 (OLQ acronym), Q13 (OLQ count)

**Total Mentions:** 5 times

**Impact:** Feels like cramming rather than natural learning

**Recommendation:**
- Session 1: Keep Q4 (OLQ intro) only
- Session 2: Remove Q9 (redundant acronym question)
- Session 2: Keep Q3 and Q13 (different angles—specific OLQs vs count)

#### 3. "PERSONAL INTERVIEW" REDUNDANCY
**Problem:** Same concept appears in Session 1 and Session 2 with similar framing

**Occurrences:**
- Session 1 Q7: "Which trait do assessors look for during Personal Interview?"
- Session 2 Q5: "During the Personal Interview, which quality matters most?"

**Impact:** Almost identical questions—poor user experience

**Recommendation:**
- Session 1: Keep Q7 (broad trait question)
- Session 2: Remove Q5 → Replace with Conference Board question

  - Options focused on problem-solving vs interpersonal
